# Banana UI Sound Effects (SFX) Guide

This folder holds audio files for Banana's UI sound effects.
All audio files must be MP3 format with standard 44.1kHz or 48kHz stereo/mono encoding.

## Sound Categories & File Names

Banana looks for the following specific sound files in this directory:

1. Category: "Scroll"
   Filename: scroll.mp3
   Event: Fired when navigating tabs, cycling exploration lenses, or scrolling lists.
   Master Volume: 0.25 (Duration ~30-60ms)
   Characteristics: Soft tactile tick or paper-friction click. Throttled at 140ms.

2. Category: "Enter"
   Filename: enter.mp3
   Event: Fired when clicking primary actions (Download MP4, Download MP3, Search, Batch Download).
   Master Volume: 0.40 (Duration ~80-150ms)
   Characteristics: Crisp Apple-like positive affirmation click.

3. Category: "Back"
   Filename: back.mp3
   Event: Fired when closing drawers, canceling operations, removing items, or closing media player.
   Master Volume: 0.35 (Duration ~70-120ms)
   Characteristics: Gentle descending tone or subtle dismiss pop.

4. Category: "Failed"
   Filename: failed.mp3
   Event: Fired when a download job fails, an invalid URL is entered, or an error occurs.
   Master Volume: 0.45 (Duration ~150-250ms)
   Characteristics: Non-jarring low alert, double-tap error tone.

5. Category: "Notification"
   Filename: notification.mp3
   Event: Fired when a download finishes, item is added to collection, or playlist loads.
   Master Volume: 0.45 (Duration ~120-250ms)
   Characteristics: Friendly glass ding, soft marimba chord.

## User-Facing Sound Toggle
Audio playback respects the user-facing sound toggle stored in `localStorage['fruit_sound_enabled']` (default ON). When disabled, sound triggers exit immediately before attempting audio initialization.

## Graceful Fallback Behavior
- If any or all MP3 files are missing, Banana continues silently with zero errors or console warnings.
